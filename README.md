# 📱 Social Media Engagement Analytics Using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing social media engagement data to understand content performance, audience behavior, posting patterns, device usage, and sentiment impact.

The analysis helps identify the factors that influence engagement, impressions, watch time, and audience interaction, providing data-driven insights that can support better social media marketing strategies.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze social media content performance.
- Identify the highest-performing post types and content categories.
- Understand the relationship between audience age and engagement.
- Compare engagement between verified and non-verified accounts.
- Identify the best time of day for maximizing impressions.
- Analyze device type and its impact on watch time.
- Evaluate how sentiment influences engagement and interaction.
- Generate actionable insights for improving social media campaigns.

# 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 1️⃣ Content Performance Analysis

### Highest Engagement Post Type

**Carousel posts** achieved the highest average engagement.

### Best-Performing Content Category

**Educational content** performed best among the analyzed content categories.

### Top Countries by Engagement Rate

The **United States, India, and Brazil** recorded the highest average engagement rates.

India also recorded the highest watch time per post among these countries.

### Business Insight

Content format and category have a strong influence on audience engagement.

### Recommendation

- Increase the use of Carousel posts.
- Produce more educational content.
- Create localized content for high-performing countries.
- Focus on content formats that encourage deeper audience interaction.

---

# 👥 2️⃣ User Trends Analysis

## Age vs. Engagement

The analysis shows differences in engagement behavior across age groups.

- Audiences aged **25–34** engage more frequently but have shorter attention spans.
- Audiences aged **35+** show fewer interactions but longer watch times.

### Business Insight

Different age groups demonstrate different engagement patterns.

### Recommendation

- Use short, interactive content for younger audiences.
- Use informative and detailed content to retain older audiences.
- Develop age-specific content strategies.

---

## Verified vs. Non-Verified Accounts

Verified accounts recorded approximately **25–30% higher engagement** compared with non-verified accounts.

### Business Insight

Account verification status is associated with higher audience engagement.

### Recommendation

- Strengthen account credibility and trust.
- Focus on building an authoritative brand presence.
- Maintain consistent and high-quality content.

---

# ⏰ 3️⃣ Behavioral Insights

## Best Time of Day for Impressions

Posts published during the **Evening** recorded the highest average impressions, with approximately **18,450 impressions**.

### Business Insight

Evening posting appears to be the most effective period for maximizing reach.

### Recommendation

- Schedule important posts during evening hours.
- Test different evening time slots.
- Monitor engagement and impressions to optimize posting schedules.

---

# 📱 4️⃣ Device Type & Watch Time

The analysis considered:

- Mobile
- Desktop
- Tablet

### Key Finding

**Mobile users recorded the highest average watch time.**

### Business Insight

Mobile users demonstrate stronger content consumption behavior.

### Recommendation

- Prioritize mobile-friendly content.
- Optimize videos and visuals for smaller screens.
- Use concise and visually engaging formats for mobile audiences.

---

# 😊 5️⃣ Sentiment Analysis

## Best-Performing Sentiment

**Positive sentiment** posts, including celebratory, motivational, and humorous content, generated the highest engagement and shareability.

### Negative & Neutral Sentiment

- **Neutral posts** encouraged more comments.
- **Negative posts** generated stronger reactions but lower overall engagement.

### Business Insight

Audience response varies significantly based on content sentiment.

### Recommendation

- Increase positive and engaging content.
- Use motivational and entertaining themes to encourage sharing.
- Use neutral content when the objective is to encourage discussion.
- Carefully manage negative content because strong reactions may not translate into higher overall engagement.
